Features

Reviews are not only opinions — they influence visibility, trust, and customer decisions. These three steps help local businesses manage feedback in a simple and structured way.

Collect reviews

After a visit, customers receive a WhatsApp message asking them to rate the service from 1 to 5. A positive rating redirects the customer to a review platform, where they are invited to leave a short comment about their experience.

Manage negative feedback

If a customer gives a low rating, they are connected directly with a manager. The business can solve the problem privately, before negative feedback appears online.

Track progress

All reviews from different platforms are collected in one dashboard in real time. Businesses can instantly see positive and negative feedback, reply immediately, and track how online ratings bring new customers from maps and review websites.
