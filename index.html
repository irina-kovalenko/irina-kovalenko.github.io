Features

Reviews are not only opinions — they influence visibility, trust, and customer decisions. These three steps help local businesses manage feedback in a simple and structured way.

Collect reviews

Customers often want to leave feedback, but the process is not always clear. This feature helps businesses collect reviews at the right moment, when customers are most willing to share their experience.

Reply faster

Negative reviews can damage trust if they stay unanswered. A fast and calm reply shows that the business cares and is ready to solve problems.

Track progress

Reviews are useful only when businesses understand the results. Tracking rating changes over time helps see what works and where improvements are needed.
